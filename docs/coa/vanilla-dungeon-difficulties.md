# Vanilla dungeon difficulties

The first playable baseline covers the 19 original dungeon maps: 33, 34, 36, 43, 47, 48, 70, 90, 109,
129, 189, 209, 229, 230, 289, 329, 349, 389 and 429. Their installed Ascension MapDifficulty.dbc provides
Normal (0), Heroic (1) and Mythic (2). Mythic+ is outside this baseline.

## Spawns and access

The existing Normal creature and gameobject spawns also participate in Heroic and Mythic. Their GUIDs,
positions, pools, events, paths and instance scripts remain unchanged. Event summons keep their original
entries, so the core selects the corresponding difficulty template without breaking scripted boss IDs.
1,278 difficulty templates in 645 families are supported by named entries in the Exiles creature export
of 13 September 2026. Models, movement, resistances, spell lists and template addons are copied with them.
AI and ScriptName remain on the original template, as required by the core.

Creature levels for all 1,278 difficulty templates use the explicit Exiles `min_level` / `max_level` values:
60 (127 variants), 61 (650), 62 (83), or 63 (418). Gordok Mastiff is 60 and VanCleef is 63 on both tiers.
The core retains the Normal entry as the creature identity; `.npc near` therefore shows the Normal ID
even when the difficulty template is active.

Boss and trash damage is described under [Boss mechanics and damage](#boss-mechanics-and-damage).
Entry requirements are inherited from the current Normal baseline. Existing higher-difficulty access rows are preserved. The baseline adds no inferred level-60 or item-level gate.

The Exiles spawn export is not a complete event implementation. Its differences include rare spawns,
seasonal actors and scripted encounters. They are not applied as wholesale spawn replacements. Exact
Ascension placement parity and the Mythic Champion's appearance rules remain unverified.

## Loot

`coa_dungeon_loot_variant` maps 999 existing equipment entries to supported Heroic and/or Mythic items.
The map is loaded at server startup and by the full loot-table reload. The original loot template rolls
its existing chances, groups, references and quantities. `Loot::AddItem` then substitutes the selected
item **before** constructing the loot item, splitting stacks and generating its random enchantments.
This also covers shared reference pools and dungeon chests without modifying those shared pools.

Only creature and gameobject loot with an actual source on one of these maps is eligible. Normal,
other maps, raids, quest-required drops, bags, mail, fishing, skinning and disenchanting keep their
original items. Missing tier mappings retain the original drop. There is no arithmetic item-ID offset.

**Provisional:** drop rates, counts and the underlying loot pools are the installed server's existing
ones, not recovered Ascension rates. AtlasLoot's newer extra boss items without supported difficulty
variants are not added. Some rare/legacy items have no verified variant and retain Normal loot.
The Exiles aggregated creature-drop table is never used as a server loot table.

## Client difficulty selection

The portrait menu calls `RequestSetDungeonDifficulty(2)` for Mythic. The local Extensions.dll
registers this function at 0x100dcdf0; it sends opcode 0x773 with one uint8 difficulty.
The early compatibility hook translates it into the queued native difficulty request, retaining
group leadership and instance restrictions. Solo changes also send the difficulty confirmation.
Dungeon Finder serves all three tiers: Random Classic Dungeon (258), Random Classic Dungeon Heroic (1258) and
Random Classic Dungeon Mythic (2258) at level 60, and the Heroic/Mythic wing entries teleport to the same start
points as Normal. The `lfg-random-classic-{heroic,mythic}-queue` scenarios queue a group and check the instance
difficulty. The Mythic gameplay scenario exercises this path; the protocol harness checks malformed payloads,
queue translation, solo confirmation and group/instance restrictions.

## Reconstructed health

`coa_dungeon_health` holds Heroic and Mythic maximum health for 1,756 creatures, keyed by map, difficulty and
original creature entry. The table is exported from an HP tool that combines user videos, combat logs and a
model: Mythic comes from the observations, Heroic is derived from Mythic (bosses and trash / 1.30, event bosses
/ 1.65). `evidence` and `source` record the origin of every row. A few later rows are corrected from in-game
checks (for example The Unforgiven, Mythic 373,000). Recount damage totals are not imported as maximum health.
Normal health is unchanged.

The engine applies these targets after template health generation, without changing creature class, damage,
faction, AI, spells or loot. Summons with an observed base entry also receive its target. Normal, pets, raids
and non-Vanilla maps are excluded. Creatures without a row keep their template health.

Targets load at world startup. The `dungeon_health` harness checks resolution and exclusions, and
`vanilla-dungeons-health` checks real spawned creatures on Normal, Heroic and Mythic, including the actual
Ascension Mythic selection request.

## Boss mechanics and damage

Each boss gets its Ascension ability kit. The target list per boss is the difficulty spell list of the Exiles
creature page, plus tier spells of the same name from the export. SmartAI bosses receive the kit as additional
`smart_scripts` rows from id 9000; where Normal, Heroic and Mythic use different tiers of a spell, event
difficulty flags pick the tier. Vanilla rows that the kit replaces get event flag 0x10, which never fires in a
5-man dungeon, so they stay in place for reference. Bosses with a C++ script keep it unchanged;
`coa_dungeon_boss_kit` runs their kit next to the script on non-raid dungeon maps.

Where main already gave a boss Ascension abilities (Deadmines bosses, Jed Runewatcher, Goraluk Anvilcrack), the
kit stays authoritative for the abilities it covers and those rows are switched off the same way; the rest of
main's logic stays (Eject Sneed, Cookie's cauldron, Goraluk's self-enchants on Normal). Edwin VanCleef's Buster
Call markers are switched off in favour of the kit's cannonball volleys at 75/50/25 %, which play the same role.
Buzzing Saw Blade (180237) keeps main's C++ AI.
Health triggers in the export are kept, cast timers come from the supplied combat logs where available and
otherwise from the closest comparable boss. Event bosses and several summons were checked in game by the
user on all 19 maps; some event bosses remain untested.

Spell damage of dungeon creatures is scaled per map and difficulty in `coa_dungeon_spell_damage`, with
fixed per-spell factors where in-game logs showed a different value. Trash spells are softened above
460 (Heroic) and 600 (Mythic) damage: the excess deals one twentieth. Bosses listed in `coa_dungeon_bosses`,
their summons and individually tuned spells are exempt. Dungeon creatures pay no power cost on Heroic and
Mythic, and creatures without health regeneration spawn at full health there.

SmartAI changes that the kits rely on, limited to dungeon creatures where noted: self-casts skip range and
line-of-sight checks, a repeat timer no longer interrupts a running channel, and a cast rejected because
another spell is in progress is retried. Ascension spell effect 174 deals a percentage of the target's maximum health or of the caster's melee damage.

Instance doors on these maps open without a key or lockpicking. Scripted doors keep opening through their
events. `CoA.BossProbe.Enable` (default off) logs boss spell damage before multipliers for tuning.
