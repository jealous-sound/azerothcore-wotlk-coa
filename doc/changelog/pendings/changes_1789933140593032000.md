### Added

- Open world scaling, per character. Every character can choose to have the open world scale to them: a
  scaled creature is shown, fought and rewarded at that character's own band (its level minus three by
  default, `DestinyWeaver.Scaling.Offset`), while the creature itself stays authored for everybody else.
  The level, health, mana, armor, damage and every level-derived roll of a fight are delivered per
  recipient, so two characters can fight the same creature at two different levels, and a character with
  scaling off keeps the original world untouched.
- Scaled quests and rewards, per character. The levelling side of the same choice:
  - the quest level a character is shown and plays at follows their own setting
    (`Player::GetQuestLevel`, through a quest-scaling resolver), instead of one realm-wide answer per
    quest;
  - quest money follows the level the quest is being played at, taken from the client's own per-level
    reward table, with a keep-share at the far end of the level range
    (`DestinyWeaver.Scaling.QuestMoneyKeepShare`, 60 by default). The reward class says nothing about
    level, so paying a rich low level quest at full strength would make trivial content the most
    profitable content; the share keeps old zones worth playing without letting them out-pay
    level-appropriate ones, and a reward is never moved below its authored value;
  - quest experience is priced at the scaled level and left whole by default
    (`DestinyWeaver.Scaling.QuestXpKeepShare`, 100), because levelling through the old zones is what the
    system is for;
  - the quest log is re-sent whenever the effective level moves, in both directions, so a scaled quest no
    longer shows grey and switching scaling off restores the authored colour;
  - the quest menu's money line and the LFG reward preview show the scaled reward;
  - kill experience, reputation and the "grants experience or honor" gates read the killer's own version
    of the creature, so a scaled kill pays what it should and a grey one still pays nothing.
- Experience bonus control, the Weaver's second service: a character can switch off the bonus experience
  sources (Potions of Experience, Auras of Experience, the recruit-a-friend bonus) and level at the base
  rate.
- The Destiny Weavers, 16 NPCs named `Destiny Weaver`, including the Stormwind and Orgrimmar weavers
  restored at their original positions and appearance, with their gossip menu, their two options and the
  notifications they send when the switch moves (individually and for a group).
- `mod-destiny-weaver`, the module that owns the character's choice and the per-viewer scaling path,
  with its own configuration file (`DestinyWeaver.*`).
- Scaling seams in the core: quest-scaling, creature-view-level and creature-view-armor resolvers in
  `LocalLevelScaling.h`, the reward keep-shares `QuestMoneyKeepSharePercent` and `QuestXpKeepSharePercent`
  with `RewardKeepPercent()`, plus `Creature::RefreshLevelDependantStats()` and
  `Player::RefreshQuestLogQueries()` for level changes at runtime, and a ceiling-free
  `ScaleCreatureLevelForViewer()` for a level that is one character's alone.
- `AscensionCompatOpcodes`, a claim/dispatch seam so a module can own one Ascension extension opcode
  rather than having CoA swallow it (`CMSG_SET_LEVEL_SCALING`).

### Changed

- Quest experience and money are priced at the level a quest is played at rather than its authored level
  whenever the character has scaling on, and `Quest::XPValue()`, `Quest::GetRewOrReqMoney()` and
  `Quest::GetRewMoneyMaxLevel()` take the caller's per-character answer.
- The realm-wide creature lift in `mod-ascension-compat` stands aside automatically while a module scales
  per viewer. That path lifts the creature object itself, which every client is told about, so the two
  models would otherwise disagree about a character who never asked for scaling. No configuration change
  is required: the takeover is read live, so a `.reload config` (or disabling the module) hands the
  answer back.

### How to upgrade

- Apply the world database updates: they add the weaver creatures, their display presets, their spawn
  rows and their gossip text, and run with the usual updater.
- Copy `modules/mod-destiny-weaver/conf/destiny_weaver.conf.dist` to your module configuration directory
  and review the `DestinyWeaver.*` keys, in particular `Scaling.QuestMoneyKeepShare` (60) and
  `Scaling.QuestXpKeepShare` (100).
- Nothing is needed on the client, and no client file or DBC is patched: a weaver's appearance is baked
  from data the server already holds and streamed to the client, so a stock client shows them.
- `CoA.LevelScaling` may be left as it is. The per-viewer module takes the answer over while
  it is enabled and gives it back when `DestinyWeaver.LevelScaling` is set to 0.
