### Fixed

- Ranks of an ability sold by the Books of Ascension can be bought in a row again. The window a book
  serves is the server's own row set, and a purchase used to invalidate it while it stayed on screen: the
  rank just learned kept its row and the rank above it stayed untrainable until the book was closed and
  reopened, so ranking an ability up to its top cost one reopen per rank. A successful purchase now
  republishes the window (`SMSG_TRAINER_LIST`) with the rank that was bought gone and the rank above it
  trainable, which is the state reopening used to produce. What the window offers is unchanged: rows are
  still the class's own ranks, still gated on the rank below and still refused above the character's level.
