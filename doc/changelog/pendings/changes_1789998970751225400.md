### Added

- The Treasure Keeper and the Celestial Treasure Keeper work again as the portable banks their own
  text describes. Both companions are realm data that was already complete (items **99491** and
  **98073**, summon spells **985356** → creature **10111377** and **93417** → creature **80918**,
  displays 48611 and 47857); what was missing was the banker flag, so a right click produced no
  packet at all. The pets now carry `UNIT_NPC_FLAG_BANKER` and the click the client sends
  (`CMSG_BANKER_ACTIVATE`, the stock opcode) is answered by the core's own banker handler with the
  character's **native** bank window — the same window a city banker opens, not this realm's separate
  personal and realm banks, which are a different feature and are untouched.
- The rule the item states is enforced as a ruleset, not as a zone: the companions are refused in
  **High-Risk** (aura 1004019) and in **War Mode** (aura 1004119 without the PvE marker), while
  **PvE** (that same aura plus the marker 9931032, which is how the client tells the two apart) and a
  character carrying no ruleset aura keep them. A **city** is exempt, because a city is not
  "High-Risk Open World": `AREA_FLAG_CAPITAL` on the character's area or zone covers Stormwind,
  Orgrimmar, the Undercity, Thunder Bluff, Darnassus, the Exodar, Silvermoon, Shattrath and Dalaran,
  and the companions work there whatever the ruleset says. Mercenary Mode needs no state of its own:
  the realm only applies it from a PvP ruleset, so a mercenary is refused with the ruleset that
  carries it.
- A companion that is **already out** is never made silent. It keeps its banker flag in every
  ruleset, so it stays right-clickable, and a click that must not open the bank is *answered* with
  the reason — in the chat log and in the middle of the screen (`SMSG_NOTIFICATION`, the
  notification opcode this realm's autobroadcasts and the weavers' messages use). A refused summon
  says the same thing with the item's own wording instead of the client's generic cast error, and an
  allowed summon says nothing at all, so normal play adds no chat.
- A keeper serves whoever clicks it. The window the core opens is the *clicking* character's own, so
  a companion another player has out opens that player's bank, under that player's ruleset and city.
  The owner is only consulted for the summon; a click that names a companion across the map is left
  to the core, so nobody is ever told about a click they did not make.
- `mod-treasure-keeper` owns the behaviour, with its own configuration file:
  `TreasureKeeper.BlockInHighRisk` and `TreasureKeeper.BlockInWarMode` (both on, which is the item
  text enforced), `TreasureKeeper.AllowInCities` (on) and `TreasureKeeper.SeparatePveFromWarMode`
  (on, which is what keeps PvE a ruleset of its own). A startup report logs both companions'
  effective display, rendered size, `npcflag` and `unit_flags`, because every way this companion can
  be wrong is silent in play — a missing banker flag answers a click with nothing, and an uncorrected
  display scale renders a pet nobody chose.
- The core gains one hook, `PlayerScript::OnPlayerBankerActivate`
  (`PLAYERHOOK_ON_BANKER_ACTIVATE`), called from `WorldSession::HandleBankerActivateOpcode` before it
  sends `SMSG_SHOW_BANK`. Returning false withholds the native window and nothing else; with no
  script listening, every banker in the world behaves exactly as before.
- Database, in `mod-treasure-keeper/data/sql/db-world/treasure_keeper.sql` and applied by the core's
  own module updater so the module and its data stay together:
  - `creature_template` entries 80918 and 10111377: `npcflag` **131072** (the banker bit *alone* — a
    unit that also carries the gossip flag is read by the client as a gossip NPC and never sends the
    banker click) and `unit_flags` **768** (IMMUNE_TO_PC | IMMUNE_TO_NPC), this realm's convention
    for its interactable companions and props.
  - `creature_template_model` entry 80918: `DisplayScale` **0.1429**, which counters display 47857's
    own 3.5× client scale to the plain keeper's half-size. The plain companion was already correct.
  - `spell_script_names`: spells **93417** and **985356** bound to `spell_treasure_keeper_summon`,
    the summon gate.
- A gameplay scenario, `apps/coa-gameplay-test/scenarios/treasure-keeper.json` — 89 steps and 44
  assertions, covering the states end to end: the PvE login default summons both companions, each
  draws at its own display and scale and is flagged as a banker, both clicks open the native bank,
  High-Risk and War Mode refuse the summon while a companion already out answers its click with the
  reason and no window, a second character's click on that companion still opens their own bank, and
  Stormwind in War Mode serves the companion and its bank. The harness gains the `banker_activate`
  and `area_trigger` actions and the `bank_shows`, `system_messages`, `cast_failure`, `pet_is_banker`,
  `pet_display` and `pet_scale` metrics.

### How to upgrade

- Apply the world database updates: the module ships its own and the core's updater applies them on
  startup, so both companions get their banker flag, their `unit_flags`, the Celestial display scale
  and the summon-spell bindings.
- Copy `modules/mod-treasure-keeper/conf/treasure_keeper.conf.dist` to your module configuration
  directory and review the `TreasureKeeper.*` keys. All four default to the item text's own
  behaviour; nothing needs to be set for the companions to work.
- Nothing is needed on the client: the bank window, the click and both displays (`CreatureDisplayInfo`
  scales 3.5 and 0.5) are the client's own data, and the Pets-tab pair is already in the realm's
  collection model table.
