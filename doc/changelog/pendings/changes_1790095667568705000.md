### Added

- Worldforged pickups now stand in every place they were recorded, not once per object
  (`2026_09_22_00_worldforged_pickup_spawns.sql`, 2,299 spawns): the pickups themselves, 3,809
  spawns over 1,534 objects and 26 maps.
  - one spawn per object **per zone** instead of the single example position the first pass
    placed it at;
  - the dungeons, mines, caves, starter sub-zones and TBC areas the community's zone dumps
    never cover, taken from the archive's own pins, which carry the object's world XYZ and map;
  - seven pickups whose map came from the two region dumps and was wrong (a Kalimdor object
    standing on the Eastern Kingdoms map), corrected to the map the zone dump and the object's
    own item pin both record;
  - the pickup holding `Adventurer's Lost Sack` (#4687), which no loot row pointed at and which
    therefore never existed on the realm at all, rebuilt field for field from the client cache;
  - `Apprentice Staff` (#4686) standing in Northshire, Mulgore and Tirisfal as well as Durotar.
- The gameplay harness can now speak about the world's own gameobjects, not only the ones a
  scenario summoned: `world_gameobject_count`, `use_world_gameobject`,
  `loot_world_gameobject`, a per-player `phase` (so a fixture can stand in the phase the placed
  object lives in), and `collect_loot` reading a gameobject's loot window as well as an item
  container's.
- `apps/coa-gameplay-test/scenarios/worldforged-pickups.json`: nine placements - an open-world
  zone, a starter sub-zone, a dungeon, a TBC zone and the object the first pass never created,
  plus four of the objects created for items no object held - each asserted to stand in the
  world, to hold exactly its own item, to hand it over once, and to hand it to a second
  character while the first has spent it.
- Worldforged pickups now exist for the 184 items no pickup object handed out
  (`2026_09_22_01_worldforged_missing_pickups.sql`): 184 objects, 461 spawns. Each item the
  archive pins on Conquest of Azeroth is placed at its own pinned position, in its own zone and
  map, and hands out exactly that item.
  - the objects carry the appearance the catalog records for them, or else the appearance this
    restoration already gives an item of the same class and subclass, and the size and lock
    every other pickup uses;
  - the 14 `Worldforged Scroll:` items are included; the `Mystic Scroll:` family is not, as
    nothing ties it to a pickup object;
  - none of those 184 items is in any creature loot, reference loot, vendor list or quest
    reward - each one is reachable through its own pickup and nowhere else;
  - 21 further objects in the realm's catalog hold an item and have no position anywhere in the
    evidence - no pin on any realm, no LootCollector sighting, no zone-dump row, no catalog
    entry, nothing in the client cache - and are left alone rather than placed at a guess.
- Pickups recorded on the other Ascension realms are placed too
  (`2026_09_22_02_worldforged_crossrealm_spawns.sql`, 76 spawns):
  - 74 recorded positions on 67 pickups the realm already had, each a place no spawn of that
    pickup stands within 30 yards of. The archive's pins carry the same world coordinates on
    Area 52, Darkmoon, Dawnrise and Bronzebeard as they do on Conquest of Azeroth, for the same
    item ids and in the same zones; the earlier passes read only the CoA ones;
  - two pickups that the client's own gameobject cache holds and that no object was ever made
    for: `Ancient Furbolg Totem` (254156), which holds `Totem of the Vale` (354068), and
    `Broken Highborne Lamp` (254193), which holds `Light of the Highborne` (354096). Both
    templates are the cache's own rows, field for field;
  - `Arkonite Orb` (254255) goes from standing nowhere to its five recorded positions: Ammen
    Vale, Azuremyst Isle, Coldridge Pass, Dun Morogh and Elwynn Forest.
- The world now holds 1,742 worldforged pickups over 4,346 placements in 30 maps, from 1,742
  objects with a loot row each.
