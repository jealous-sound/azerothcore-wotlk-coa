### Changed

- Open-world level scaling follows the client's own data. Weapons and armor with an `ItemStat.dbc` level
  ladder keep their entry and take a scaling level when looted, won or rewarded (`CoA.ItemScaling.Native`),
  stored in the new `character_item_scaling` table. An item whose own armor matches the ladder's Reborn
  column at its item level wears that column at every level. While native item scaling is active the old
  copy lift (`CoA.ItemScaling`) makes no new copies; copies already handed out keep working. Scaled
  dungeons hold their creatures at the character's own level inside the dungeon finder band
  (`DestinyWeaver.Scaling.DungeonIds`), and the open-world offset is now 4 on the maps in
  `DestinyWeaver.Scaling.WorldMaps`.
- A quest with a row in `QuestTemplateScaling.dbc` trails the character inside that row's minimum and
  maximum level instead of being raised to the character's level. Experience is priced at that level,
  money by its tier's ratio between that level and the quest's own; neither is lowered below the
  authored reward, and a lifted quest that grants no experience still grants none. Quests without a row
  keep their authored level.
- Challenge rules `NO_CREATURE_LEVEL_SCALING` and `NO_QUEST_LEVEL_SCALING` (`ChallengeRuleTypes.dbc`) hold
  the character's creatures and quests at their authored levels while the challenge is active.
- War Mode (without PvE Mode) and High-Risk switch level scaling off in the open world, as their tooltips
  state (`CoA.Ruleset.DisableLevelScaling`).
- A scaled creature's melee blows spread over its whole damage range instead of a few flat values, and its daze
  chance and the weapon skill a character gains from it follow the level that character is shown.
- Master-looted scaling gear takes the receiver's level. Native item scaling stores the client's item key
  for the level and levels quest rewards by the quest's own curve, and shows each character the version of
  an item they would receive in the loot window, roll frames, inspect, mail and auctions. Items stored
  before this change keep their plain level.
- Balance change: worn native-scaled armor gives every class except Hero its reborn armor, as the client's
  tooltip shows, instead of full armor. The client evidence shows the display, not that Ascension's server
  applied it. Median reborn/full armor is 0.25 for cloth, 0.40 for leather, 0.71 for mail and 0.98 for plate,
  so cloth, leather and mail wearers lose most of their item armor. Items whose `ItemAddon.dbc` reborn armor
  is 0 keep their own armor. `CoA.ItemScaling.Native.RebornArmor = 0` restores full armor.

### Removed

- `CoA.ItemScaling.Native.LevelKeys` and `CoA.ItemScaling.Native.Preview`: item keys and the preview are
  always on while native item scaling is. Against `main` both defaulted to 0, so a realm that never set
  them now stores item keys and shows the preview.
- `DestinyWeaver.Scaling.QuestMoneyKeepShare` and `DestinyWeaver.Scaling.QuestXpKeepShare`: the quest's own
  curve bounds its rewards now.

### How to upgrade

- Delete `DestinyWeaver.Scaling.QuestMoneyKeepShare` and `DestinyWeaver.Scaling.QuestXpKeepShare` from
  `destiny_weaver.conf`; review `DestinyWeaver.Scaling.Offset` (now 4), `DestinyWeaver.Scaling.WorldMaps`
  and `DestinyWeaver.Scaling.DungeonIds`.
- Add `CoA.Ruleset.DisableLevelScaling`, `CoA.QuestLevelScaling.ClientFlag` and the
  `CoA.ItemScaling.Native*` keys to `coa.conf` from `coa.conf.dist`.
- Place the client's `QuestTemplateScaling.dbc` in the server's `dbc` folder; without it no quest scales.
