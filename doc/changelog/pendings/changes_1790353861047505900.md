### Fixed

- **Westfall is revised: the zone walked by hand in the map editor and then reviewed row by row on its
  own sheet** (`2026_09_25_54_worldforged_westfall_revision.sql`). This is not a marker pairing - the
  eleven earlier passes took the zone from the realm's own map page - but a pass over the zone as it
  stands: **41 changesets** written in the client's editor (the pro2 project) between 17:41 and 19:03
  on 2026-09-25, and the zone's sheet of `worldforged-items.xlsx` marked up by hand afterwards, **41
  rows green** (kept) and **45 red** (out). The file is both halves read together, and where they
  disagree the review wins.
- **The walk re-placed 34 rows and took 4 objects out of the client's own data, and placed 3 objects
  again on fresh guids.** Of the 41 rows the review kept, **35 were re-placed in the walk** - 33 moved
  and/or turned by the editor, and 2 whose object it emptied at its old spot and placed again
  elsewhere in the zone, which take the guid the editor allocated: **6960037** for Defias Mage Stash
  in Moonbrook (entry 90252, from its Longshore spot 1,187 yd away) and **6960038** for Harvest Golem
  Scythe at The Dead Acre (entry 1345068, from The Molsen Farm) - and **5 were kept exactly as they
  stood** (Defias Toe Knife, Field Boots, Flexible Gloves, Stolen Thunderbrew Flask, Worn Quel'thalas
  Heirloom). Position and rotation quaternion are what the editor emitted, down to the digit, and
  `orientation` is the yaw that quaternion represents. The zone's sheet now carries **40 rows**: the
  forty-first, the **Deadman's Signet** (515510), was moved out of the zone in the same walk, out of
  Alexston Farmstead into **Raven Hill Cemetery in Duskwood**, and its row went with it to that zone's
  sheet, in walk order - the one row the walk took out of Westfall without the review taking it out of
  the world.
- **45 rows are taken out of the zone, 48 objects deleted for them**, every one of the 45 marked red
  on the sheet by hand: the three rows whose objects the editor emptied and placed again are green, so
  their old spots go too - 6940322 (Deadman's Signet), 6940347 (Defias Mage Stash) and 6940594
  (Harvest Golem Scythe). 44 distinct object entries stand behind the 45 rows. **The review has a
  shape**: every row it kept holds an item of **level 16 to 25**, and **43 of the 45 it took out hold
  items of level 31 to 58** - the two exceptions being Defias Magus Staff (level 18, which the editor
  had also nudged in the same walk, and a removal is the last thing said about a row, so the nudge is
  not written) and Rehomed Belt (level 25).

  spawns in 62 areas** across the world - Westfall's own 45 of them, then Longshore 9, Teldrassil 5,
  Thousand Needles 5, Swamp of Sorrows 3, Arathi Highlands 3, Darkcloud Pinnacle 3 and the rest - and
  **every one of them outside the zone is left exactly as it was**, in the world and in the book: the
  realm-map design gives each object one placement per realm map, and this zone gave up only its own.
  Of the workbook's twenty-three sheets only two changed, the zone's own and Duskwood's.

  wherever their own realm maps put them.
