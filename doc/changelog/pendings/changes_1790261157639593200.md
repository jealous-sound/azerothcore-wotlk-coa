### Fixed

- The Worldforged pickups of **Deadwind Pass** are checked placement by placement in the map editor,
  and the pass is written as the editor emitted it: **22 rows moved**, **13 of them turned**, the
  furthest **Scorched Tome** (914 yd), **Heirloom of the Whisperwind** (285), **Cellar Rubble**
  (168), **Unique Bush** (149) and **Celestial Edge of Starfall** (89); the largest turns **Red
  Plumage** (3.08 rad), **Cursed Branch** (2.95), **Timberbane's Old Hatchet** (2.93) and **Blade of
  the Faithful** (2.57). Each row carries the position and the rotation quaternion exactly as the
  editor wrote them, and the orientation column as the yaw that quaternion represents; nothing else
  in a row is touched, so spawn masks, phase, loot table and the comment history survive and the
  file is idempotent.
- Five objects whose rows the editor removed - **Forgotten Cane**, **Small War Drum**, **Lunar
  Tome**, **Wooden Maul** and **Stuck Scimitar** - are placed again where the editor put them, on
  fresh guids, each keeping the loot row its object already had. The zone's **Timberling Ritual
  Blade** is placed the same way, on the spot the editor authored for it.
- **Eight rows are removed in all**, six of them by the editor's own changesets and two more on the
  walk through the zone afterwards, each of the last two a second row of an object the pass had just
  answered for: the **Timberling Ritual Blade** placed at -11156.9 -2479.2 still had its old row
  standing on the realm map's own listing of it, and the **Shining Wand** is the item the map draws
  on two pages, its Deadwind Pass listing and the listing on the page for the zone below it, both of
  whose rows are now gone. That item is handed out by no pickup of this module.
- Every one of the rows was read back from the database afterwards and matches the file exactly: the
  22 rewritten rows, the 6 added placements, and none of the 8 removed guids remains. The module's
  totals move by the two rows removed on the walk: 2,518 pickups over 1,621 distinct ids.
