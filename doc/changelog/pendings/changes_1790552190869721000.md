### Removed

- The realm-wide creature level scaling in CoA. It raised the creature object itself, which every client
  is told about, so one creature had one level for everybody: the nearest character, or a playerbot,
  decided it for the whole area. Open-world creature scaling is now only the Destiny Weaver's per-character
  view (`mod-destiny-weaver`), where a level 30 and a level 20 character see the same level 15 creature at
  27 and 17. With `DestinyWeaver.Enable` or `DestinyWeaver.LevelScaling` at 0, creatures keep their
  authored level instead of falling back to a realm-wide lift.
- The `CoA.LevelScaling` and `CoA.LevelScalingMaxLift` configuration keys. `CoA.QuestLevelScaling` is
  unchanged.
- The gameplay harness's `level_scaling` creature fixture option, which only opted a fixture into the
  realm-wide lift.

### How to upgrade

- Delete `CoA.LevelScaling` and `CoA.LevelScalingMaxLift` from `coa.conf`; they are no longer read.
