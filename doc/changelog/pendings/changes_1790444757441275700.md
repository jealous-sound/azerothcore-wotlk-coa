# Modules: mod-battlehorn, mod-scrolls-of-retreat
##################################

## CoA vanity collectibles: the Battle Horn and the four Retreat and Defense scrolls

Five items the client and the live realm both know could not exist on a realm built from this
repository, and the four scrolls could not land anywhere. `Item::CreateItem()` returns null for an
item that has no `item_template` row, and every one of the four teleports is
`SPELL_EFFECT_TELEPORT_UNITS` aimed at `TARGET_DEST_DB` - a destination the server reads out of
`spell_target_position` - so with no row there the scroll paid its cooldown and left the caster
standing still.

| item | name | what was missing |
|---|---|---|
| 898070 | Battle Horn | `item_template` row |
| 101257 | Scroll of Defense: Ashenvale | `item_template` row, destination for 83126 |
| 101258 | Scroll of Defense: Hillsbrad Foothills | `item_template` row, destination for 83128 |
| 1175626 | Scroll of Retreat: Stormwind | destination for 84289 (item restored by the warchest file) |
| 1175627 | Scroll of Retreat: Orgrimmar | destination for 84288 (item restored by the warchest file) |

The Battle Horn's pool row, `(898070, 3015, 350, 0)`, already stands in mod-ethereal-bazaar's
generated `03_ethereal_bazaar_pool.sql`, taken from the live vendor's own rotation, and was dangling
until now: the rotation slot came up empty. The two Scrolls of Defense are pooled here at the same
350-token tier, which is where the live realm sells them, and the two Scrolls of Retreat keep their
warchest source.

Two modules own the work, so that anything either item needs later - a spell script, an added
cooldown - has an obvious home. Their SQL sits in `data/sql/updates/pending_db_world/`, where the
realm's other module data lives as well (the Stones of Retreat's destinations, the warchest), as two
new files: `rev_20260926_80_battle_horn_item.sql` and
`rev_20260926_81_retreat_and_defense_scrolls.sql`.

* **`mod-battlehorn`** - the horn's item row, all 138 columns, field for field what a repack
  database carries. Spell 898070 itself is the client's: `SPELL_EFFECT_THREAT` on every enemy
  within 30 yd plus a DUMMY effect, both of which the core implements, so the module is data only.
* **`mod-scrolls-of-retreat`** - the four `spell_target_position` rows, the two Scrolls of Defense
  item rows and their two pool rows, plus one script: the faction gate on the two capital scrolls.
  Destinations are the server's own `.tele` hub points, the convention
  `rev_20260916_00_stone_of_retreat_destinations.sql` already uses, and the two capitals reuse the
  landings that file audited: Orgrimmar as 777000, Stormwind as 777003 with its z lifted onto the
  walkable floor.

On the live realm each capital scroll is kept to its own side, and that half is not expressible as
data: `item_template` cannot say "this faction", and the client's own refusal for the fields that
exist would be its generic red cast error rather than a line about the scroll. So **1175626 Scroll of
Retreat: Stormwind is Alliance only** and **1175627 Scroll of Retreat: Orgrimmar is Horde only** are
answered in code, in the shape mod-treasure-keeper answers its own ruleset refusal with: one line to
the middle of the screen and to the chat log at once, in the realm's notification yellow with the
faction painted in its colour - `|cff4c9aff` Alliance, `|cffff2020` Horde - and the chat copy carries
the scroll as a real, clickable item link. A refused scroll is never consumed and takes no cooldown,
because the core asks `ScriptMgr::OnItemUse` before it casts a used item; the line itself is held to
one announcement per three seconds per character, so a repeated request cannot stack it. The gate is an
`AllItemScript` keyed on the two item entries and not an `ItemScript` reached through
`item_template.ScriptName`, so a later `REPLACE` of those rows - they belong to the warchest file -
cannot quietly switch it off. It also stays clear of the realm's other faction rule, mod-teleport-actionbar's generated hub-side table for the Stones of Retreat, which is keyed by the stone teleport spells: the scrolls' two spells are not in it and their refusal sentence is their own.

The two Scrolls of Retreat items stay in
`rev_20260924_10_coa_warchest_item_templates.sql`, where the rest of the welcome warchest's grant
list lives; two files writing the same row would be two places to keep in step.

Every statement is idempotent and written as `REPLACE`, which replaces the row with that exact key
and touches nothing else - `item_template` is never deleted from. Both files pass
`apps/codestyle/codestyle-sql.py --files <path>` and a structural check of their own (138 columns
against 138 values, and the smaller tables against theirs), and both were applied twice against a
live world database, with every row read back identical to the one the repack shipped.

### How to upgrade

Build and apply the two new SQL files. `mod-battlehorn` and the scrolls' data are database only, but
`mod-scrolls-of-retreat` ships the faction gate as code (`src/scrolls_of_retreat.cpp`), so the realm
has to be rebuilt for it to be in - the SQL on its own changes no behaviour. Both files are recorded
in `updates` and applied again whenever they change.

### What to test in game

* use each of the four scrolls and land where its `spell_target_position` row says;
* the two Scrolls of Retreat are reusable, the two Scrolls of Defense are spent on use and leave the
  teleport in the spellbook;
* try the Stormwind scroll on a Horde character and the Orgrimmar scroll on an Alliance one: the
  refusal has to be read in the middle of the screen and in the chat log, the scroll has to stay in
  the bag with no cooldown on it, and the scroll's own faction has to use it as before;
* hover the two Scrolls of Retreat: the cooldown against them is the item rows' twelve hours;
* blow the Battle Horn next to enemies: the threat has to reach everything within 30 yards, and the
  horn ability has to survive the horn itself, which is spent by the use.
