### Fixed

- The Worldforged pickups of **Silverpine Forest** are judged against the realm's own map page
  marker by marker and then walked by hand in the map editor, and both passes are written as they
  ran: **three second placings added** and **seven objects restored** by the marker pass, **25 rows
  moved and/or turned**, **13 rows removed** and **5 placements added** by the walk. Every position
  and every rotation quaternion in the files is the realm's own or the editor's own, digit for
  digit, and the orientation column is the yaw that quaternion represents. The zone stands **38
  pickups**.
- The marker pass reads the zone's **46 placements** over its twenty-six areas and finds nothing
  off its own mark, so nothing is moved. It adds a second placing for **Drowned Adventurer**
  (101911), **Forgotten Book of Healing** (99010) and **Light and Shadow Vol: 1** (100011);
  restores seven objects this world never carried, field for field from the realm's own client-cache
  records - **Ancient Priest Tome** (101912), **Codex of Divine Mending** (99018), **Cold Crystal**
  (101914), **Shadow Portal** (111017), **Sorcerer's Cache** (835958), **The Law of Light** (111004,
  a second book of that name, with the loot row for the item its marker names, 9200812) and
  **Maddening Aura** (101913); stands the zone's own drop listing over Fenris Isle on the realm's own
  recorded spots - **Gnoll Subdue Wand** (90312), **Rot Hide Stash** (90313) and **Cursed Fang
  Remains** (90314); and removes one duplicate row, **Meat Wagon Small Claw** (guid 6930016).
- The walk afterwards moves **25 rows** - the furthest **Turtle Shell** 489.3 yd, **Sorcerer's
  Cache** 152.5, **Siren's Wand** 137.8, **Deadman's Dagger** 60.4 and **Dirt Covered Gown** 46.7 -
  and turns ten of them, the largest **Grimson Cloak** (1.207 rad), **Siren's Wand** (0.908) and
  **Dirt Covered Gown** (0.781). Five objects are taken out of their old spots and placed again
  where the editor put them, on fresh guids and each keeping the loot row its object already had:
  **Old Shoulderpad** (517314, guid 6960022), **Hidden Ring** (97100, 6960023), **Well Kept
  Hatchet** (95946, 6960024), **Heavy Shovel** (515383, 6960025) and **A "Fishy" Staff** (95947,
  6960026).
- **Six of the thirteen removals are the Mystic Scroll props the marker pass had just restored** -
  **Shadow Portal**, **The Law of Light**, **Light and Shadow Vol: 1**, **Codex of Divine
  Mending**, **Forgotten Book of Healing** and **Maddening Aura**. Each pairs at another of its own
  listings with the scroll the realm's own loot pin beside it names - Shadow Reserves, Long Arm of
  the Law, Light and Shadow, and Promise of Renewal - and the Mystic Scroll family is deliberately
  outside this module's scope. **Ivar's Femur** (90324) and **Rot Hide Supplies** (90317) are
  removed as well and placed nowhere else.
- Nothing that is not this module's is touched: the same changesets also move eighteen objects the
  world itself owns around Ambermill - Cozy Fires, Wooden and High Back Chairs, the Ambermill
  Strongbox, Bruiseweed and Mageroyal - and write ten creature rows. Every one of them is left
  exactly as it stands.
- Every row was read back from the live database afterwards and matches the files exactly: **257
  assertions, none failing** - the marker pass's own 166 over the final state and the authored
  file's 91, covering each row's position, quaternion, the yaw it represents, its entry and script,
  each placement, and the absence of every removed guid. Both passes are idempotent: applied again
  they leave the same rows. The realm stands **2,538 pickups over 1,639 distinct ids**, **1,805
  pickup templates** and **99,475 gameobjects**.
