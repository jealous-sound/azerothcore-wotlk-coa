## mod-worldforged-pickups: Westfall, four pickups restored from the reference realm's own placement

A gameobject dump of the live reference realm stands thirty worldforge pickups in Westfall; twenty of
them this world already stood within a few yards of where the dump puts them. Of the ten it held that
the zone did not, four are the Travel Sack and the Defias Magus Staff the review of 2026-09-25 took
out on purpose and four are books and chests earlier passes had restored into other zones. All three
of those stand empty - no loot row at all - and are left out.

`2026_09_25_55_worldforged_westfall_missing.sql` restores the four loot-bearing pickups the zone was
missing, each at the reference realm's own coordinates, none handing out a mystic scroll:

* Salma's Summer Wardrobe (95777) at Saldean's Farm -> Salma's Summer Dress (515677), at the spot and
  with the rotation the map editor re-authored the same evening;
* Unlocked Chest (95809) at Saldean's Farm -> Field Trousers (450775);
* Heavy Stompers (95817) at Stendel's Pond -> Heavy Boots (450721);
* Stolen Supplies (518322) by Sentinel Hill -> Rehomed Belt (521266).

An earlier draft of the file also stood The Law of Light (111000) in The Dagger Hills; it is not
wanted and the file as it now stands takes that spawn back out. Every template and loot row is
restated in the file, so it stands alone. Idempotent; verified by applying it twice by hand and once
more through the worldserver's boot path, then reading every claim back from the database: 61
assertions, none failing. The realm reads 99,435 gameobjects and 2,781 worldforged pickups. The
zone's sheet of worldforged-items.xlsx follows at 44 rows.
