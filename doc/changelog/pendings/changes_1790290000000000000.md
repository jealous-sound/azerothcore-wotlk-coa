    records it: The Wanderer's Stirring Rod, Thunder Falls Finest Gun Selection, Loose Stone, the second
    Barrel Smasher the map pass had stood at the map's own marker (the placement authored in game is the
    one that remains), and the two extra Echo Ridge Mine placements the earlier authored pass added.

    and/or turned, and five placements the author placed himself: Morale Boosters, Scorched Tome Rock
    Smasher, the Lost Shipment chest that hands out Charred Spaulders, and the Gypsy Wagon prop beside
    the Traveler's Forest Cloak.
