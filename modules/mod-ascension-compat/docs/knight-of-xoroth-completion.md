# Knight of Xoroth completion policy — 2026-09-09

This package reconstructs the local class from the pinned copied-client records and the recovered
changelog. It is source ready; a linked server build, installation and gameplay acceptance are separate.
The 124 original findings have 122 implemented/extended dispositions and two retained native mechanisms.
See the [release history](local-release-state.md) for later integration.

## Resources and attacks

Demonfire remains a six-stack aura. Eleven spender families require at least one stack and reserve
the current amount before effects. A triggered helper inherits that cast's snapshot. New stacks earned
by its damage/procs remain available; the old delayed removers 520440/520441 are disabled. Completed
attacks whose hostile targets all miss, dodge or parry refund the spent amount, capped at six. Immunity,
an empty target set or any successful target prevents this refund. Bellows combines both cones.
The recovered changelog record 69912 (2026-08-01) supplies the miss-refund rule. Blood Frenzy and Stoke
apply their refunds only to non-refunded spending. Blood and Demonfire are independent resources.

Skulltaker and Warbringer gain 20% and 18% damage per Demonfire respectively, following record 66622
(2025-12-22). The other recovered bundle rules are explicit: Flames +50% damage per stack, Hellmaw
+20% direct/periodic damage, Implosion +100% damage and one additional imp, Melt -4% armor, Suffuse
three seconds, Bellows three seconds of cooldown reduction, and Hellrider +20% amount and duration.
Hellstorm extends Embers by its base duration per stack. The parent Bellows cast carries the behind
helper's authored 20 Rage/15-second base cooldown; both cones use the current parent damage formula.
Per-target Bellows hits reduce Chains of Xoroth cooldown by four seconds. No legacy delayed remover
may wipe the stacks generated by those hits. Hand of Doom maintains its native base weapon modifier
plus ten points per active Demonfire; Splinters and Curse use their recovered stack-based proc chances.

Juggernaut and active Black Shield consume only Demon's Blood. Their healing/absorb gains 20% per
reserved Blood stack. Juggernaut retains the current copied-client +0.1 AP term despite an older
archived removal notice. The active Black Shield uses its base absorb; only Curse of Xoroth's next
Shieldgore grants the additional +2 Stamina term. Sieger doubles either shield below 35% health.
Suffuse retains base 200 plus 10% Stamina, 5% AP and 20% defense rating, using the explicit current
tooltip and record 69189 (2026-07-23). Defense rating means the rating field, not total defense skill.

The coefficient table has 65 exact effect slots, including separate Hellmaw direct/periodic terms,
owned summon Firebolt/Imp Rush and the described triggered helpers. SQL removes stock bonus coefficients
for these rows and actual-result copies. Shieldgore adds block value one for one; its exact private
coefficient was not recovered, so this is an explicit local choice. Existing rank base points and native
level scaling remain. Sever's native 142-to-804079 value forwarding is retained for all ten ranks.

## Finite effects and proc ownership

Fiend, Gorged, Demonic Blade, Infernal Steel, Xorothian Fury/Rage, Decimation, the Curse shield and
Annihilation select only the intended next ability. A refreshed aura receives a new generation so
an earlier cast cannot remove it. Fiend makes Infernal/Shieldgore free and does not grant its stale,
unadvertised damage bonus. Burning Blade consumes one of six plus spent-Demonfire attacks on each
landed direct melee attack; only critical attacks emit its Fire line. Demonic Blade has two Gore uses
and casts a free learned Meatsaw on consumption or natural expiry, not dispel/death. Decimation has
five PvE uses or one player-targeted use, including a missed player-targeted attack. It guarantees a
critical hit and applies +100%/+25% damage respectively. Xorothian Rage also guarantees its selected hit.

Gorged heals 50% of actual Gore damage over three seconds. At three stacks its next Sever/Bludgeon
leeches 200% of the actual hit from up to five nearby enemies, consumes the stacks and repeats once
after one second. Sever's cooldown increases by two seconds; Bludgeon's by twelve. The repeat is a
free triggered cast and does not create another repeat or consume a second selected buff. Stoke and
Maw copy actual results without another critical roll or a second set of damage modifiers. Hellmaw's
Maw accumulator detonates on natural expiry; spread recipients start their own accumulator.

Doom retains the last six auto-attack contributions in its refreshed DoT. Each contributes 10% of its
actual damage over the authored ticks. Ascended Warlock uses a local 50% copy of actual Skulltaker
damage; the recovered description only says reduced damage. Copied direct results cannot recursively
start the same damage chains. Omen of Rage may still roll on effective periodic healing, including
Gorged. Proc direction, actual damage/healing, school, owner, avoidance and finite cooldown gates are
checked explicitly. The native HIT proc rows merely admit the appropriate events to these checks.

## Fields, defense, summons and travel

Locally, one Pestilence is active per owner and one Mark per caster per target; other casters' Marks
remain. Conquest, Famine and Apocalypse refresh short-lived owned effects around the Knight. Death
retains its native critical bonus; War heals per spent Demonfire. Unleash uses the active Pestilence,
nearby valid enemies and the authored helper. Half-strength Unleash halves damage/heal/absorb amounts;
DoT duration is retained so total damage is halved once. Famine control duration and Conquest's native
school lock are halved. A general interrupt-duration hook adjusts the latter after native admission
and target duration modifiers; other interrupts retain their existing duration.

Impish Pestilence has a ten-second application gate. Its four recovered Lesser Pestilences are exclusive
on the permanent Greater Imp. With no distinct recovered Death pet helper, Death selects the War
helper locally. Evil Duo unleashes the pet's chosen effect around the pet. Unleashes affect at most
ten enemies within ten yards; Unbound Inferno uses an eight-yard area around the struck victim.
Native immunity, phase and target checks remain; several reconstructed area limits are local choices.

Legion's Presence grants raid damage, plus the separate imp Fire/health bonuses. Demonic Grit reduces
incoming damage and increases summon health. Infernal Bulwark absorbs 20% of the damage left after a
real block. Resolve updates Strength from half the block rating, retaining native Strength-to-hit
rating. Live owned imp GUIDs determine block, Strength, damage reduction, critical-block chance and
the Abyssal Rage discount. Blood stacks update the owner's Buckler health bonus.

The unrecovered private fierce-blow system uses a scoped local substitute: while Forgemaster is active,
a Knight with a usable shield can convert an otherwise crushing frontal melee result into a block
with the recovered 60% chance. This does not increase ordinary block chance or change other classes.
The Demon's Blood trait changes the Knight's creature type to Demon for native targeting checks.

The Greater Imp is a native permanent summon pet, with 80 guarded level-stat rows copied from native
Imp 416 and Firebolt/Imp Rush on its action bar. Temporary imps and Abyssals have owned AI and follow
or assist their master. Local baseline health is 25%/60% of owner maximum health; temporary weapon
damage is level*1.5 + 10% owner AP, and armor follows the owner. Applicable owner bonuses are applied
once. Imps redirect their threat to the owner. Implosion summons one plus reserved Demonfire; native
forwarded values retain Imp Gang Boss's extra Call imps. Each cast is capped at twelve summons.
Flay consumes an eligible Humanoid/Beast/Demon corpse out of combat and heals the player, permanent
pet and owned nearby temporary combat summons. Chains of Xoroth pull eligible nearby enemies at expiry.

Seven guarded creature/model definitions use existing installed displays: Greater Imp/Hellfire Imp
4449 at scales 0.85/0.55, Abyssal 169 at 0.7, chain anchor 11686, portal 9510, Warsteed 14554 and
Doomguard 68. The last four use scale 1. These are native model fallbacks, not recovered official
appearances. No shared model or DBC row is edited. Forge/Anvil use native displays 209/273 and crafting
focus data. Installation must reject conflicting template/model/pet-level/gameobject definitions.

Warsteed generates Path only after three seconds without dealing/taking damage. The stack cap is
two below level 30, three from 30 and five from 40. Damage, Momentum, Bloodrider, Stirrups and dismount
are handled. Hellbound has two sixty-second charges and forbids full mounted-combat abilities during
its short ride. Path scales mounted attacks and the native forward jump; the local jump scaling
affects the native speed parameter and needs in-game movement acceptance. The portal accepts its owner
or raid members within five yards, alive and out of combat.
